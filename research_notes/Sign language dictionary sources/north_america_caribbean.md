# Sign-language dictionary sources: North America and the Caribbean (ASL extras, LSQ, Indigenous, Caribbean)

Research date: 2026-10-14.

**How licenses were checked.** Licenses were verified on original pages or through official JSON APIs:
- Kaggle page/API
- OSF API
- Commons/Wiktionary API
- Glottolog JSON
- archive.org metadata
- HathiTrust API
- loc.gov JSON
- Borealis API
- ELAR pages

Licenses are quoted verbatim. Unverified items are marked UNVERIFIED.

**Downloads.** The only things downloaded were under 20 MB: the MS-ASL zip (1.9 MB), SPML dumps, license PDFs and one badge image.

**Search budget.** The session's shared WebSearch budget ran out partway through, so several leads were checked only by direct URL fetches. Non-English (Spanish, French, Kreyòl) searches for the Caribbean and LSQ could not be run.

**Tiers:**
- A: open (CC BY / BY-SA / CC0 / PD).
- B: non-commercial or research-only.
- C: registration, contract or permission.
- D: skip.

## Q1. ASL (ase): which sources beyond Marionet's current set are usable, and what are their verified licenses?

### Takeaway
Three new ASL sources pass Tier A, all verified on the original page. (1) **Google Isolated Sign Language Recognition (Kaggle `asl-signs`)**: MediaPipe landmarks for 250 signs, CC BY 4.0 under the competition rules, plus a CC0 rerun set. (2) **PopSign ASL v1.0**: videos of the same 250 signs, CC BY 4.0, 1.1 TB. (3) **Wiktionary's 271 ASL lemma pages**: CC BY-SA, written in a "Handshape@Location-Facing + Move" notation that maps almost one-to-one onto SignDesc. The extra SignPuddle US puddles (sgn25, sgn28, …) are also Tier A. Google's fingerspelling releases (FSboard; Kaggle `asl-fingerspelling`) are CC BY 4.0. Everything else is Tier B, C or D:
- **ASL Citizen:** the Microsoft Research License Terms, non-commercial and with no redistribution. The CC BY label in the dataset hub is wrong.
- **Sem-Lex:** CC BY-NC-SA 4.0.
- **MS-ASL:** C-UDA 0.1, with YouTube URLs.
- **ASLLVD, ASLLRP Sign Bank and NCSLGR:** BU terms, research and education only, no redistribution.
- **Purdue RVL-SLLL:** a signed agreement.
- **NVIDIA Signs:** a purpose-limited contract.
- **Handspeak, Lifeprint, Signing Savvy, SignASL and ASL Clear:** proprietary.

**There is a new conflict in a source Marionet already uses.** The asl-lex.org download page now says ASL-LEX is CC BY-NC 4.0 and "Use of any part of ASL-LEX for commercial purposes is expressly prohibited." The OSF node that Marionet ingests still declares CC BY 4.0.

### Cited Findings

**Tier A: new, usable**

- **Google – Isolated Sign Language Recognition (Kaggle `asl-signs`)**
  - **Language:** ase.
  - **Type:** pose and landmarks (MediaPipe Holistic 0.9.0.1, parquet), not video.
  - **Size:** "hand and facial landmarks generated by Mediapipe version 0.9.0.1 on ~100k videos of isolated signs performed by 21 Deaf signers from a 250-sign vocabulary." The size of the main competition download was not verified.
  - **License:**
    - The Kaggle API license object reads `{'name': 'Subject to Competition Rules'}`.
    - The rules header reads "**DATA ACCESS AND USE**: CC-BY 4.0".
    - §7.A: "*Competition Use and Commercial*: You may access and use the Competition Data for any purpose, whether commercial or non-commercial … The Competition Data is also subject to the following terms and conditions: CC-By 4.0. To the extent that the terms and conditions located at the URL conflict with or are inconsistent with these Rules, these Rules will govern".
    - **Caveat:** §7.B also says "You agree not to transmit, duplicate, publish, redistribute or otherwise provide or make available the Competition Data to any party not participating in the Competition."
    - The Acknowledgements page reads: "The dataset provided by Deaf Professional Arts Network and the Georgia Institute of Technology is licensed under CC-BY 4.0. Kaggle and Google do not own and have not validated the dataset in any way."
  - **SPDX:** CC-BY-4.0. **Tier:** A for derived SignDesc; do not re-host the raw parquet (because of §7.B).
  - **Access:** Kaggle account, accept the rules, then `kaggle competitions download -c asl-signs`.
  - **commercial_ok:** yes. **video_redistrib_ok:** not applicable (no video).
  - **Citation:** Google / DPAN / Georgia Tech, "Google – Isolated Sign Language Recognition", Kaggle 2023.
  - **Next action:** this is the first new ASL ingest. Fit 3D hand landmarks to SignDesc hand tuning for the 250 signs, and join glosses to ASL-LEX.
  - Sources: Kaggle rules, data description, Acknowledgements and Data Card, all retrieved via Kaggle's page API for competition 46105 ([competition](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/asl-signs); [rules](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/asl-signs/rules); [data](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/asl-signs/data)).
- **ISLR "Rerun Set"**
  - Kaggle dataset `sohier/461054610546105`, titled "ASL Isolated Sign Recognition Rerun Set". It is linked from the competition data page: "the rerun dataset has been published here."
  - **License field:** "CC0: Public Domain". **Size:** 19,844,428,879 bytes.
  - **Tier:** A. This is the cleanest way to get ISLR-style landmarks without the §7.B tension.
  - Source: [Kaggle dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/sohier/461054610546105), metadata via the public Kaggle API `datasets/view`.
- **PopSign ASL v1.0** (Georgia Tech / NTID / Google)
  - **Language:** ase. **Type:** isolated video (MP4), recorded on smartphone selfie cameras.
  - **Size:** 250 signs, 47 signers, 200,686 videos (165,198 game videos), 1.1 TB.
  - **License:** "Popsign v1.0 is licensed under CC BY 4.0."
  - **SPDX:** CC-BY-4.0. **Tier:** A.
  - **Access:** open download from signdata.cc.gatech.edu.
  - **commercial_ok:** yes. **video_redistrib_ok:** yes, with attribution (right-of-publicity caveat as for FSboard).
  - **Citation:** Starner et al., "PopSign ASL v1.0", NeurIPS 2023 Datasets & Benchmarks.
  - **Next action:** pull only a few per-sign clips as citation-form references for the 250 ISLR signs. Do not mirror 1.1 TB.
  - Sources: [Georgia Tech data page](https://signdata.cc.gatech.edu/view/datasets/popsign_v1_0/index.html); [NeurIPS supplement](https://papers.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2023/file/00dada608b8db212ea7d9d92b24c68de-Supplemental-Datasets_and_Benchmarks.pdf).
  - A Kaggle re-upload, `mrgeislinger/popsign-asl-v1-0-game-test` (131.7 GB), is labelled "CC BY-SA 4.0". Treat it as a mirror and use the original's CC BY 4.0 ([Kaggle API](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mrgeislinger/popsign-asl-v1-0-game-test)).
- **FSboard** (Google, fingerspelling)
  - **Type:** continuous fingerspelling video.
  - **Size:** more than 3 million characters, more than 250 hours, 147 Deaf signers. Kaggle `googleai/fsboard`: totalBytes 1,451,641,027,485.
  - **License:** "Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)" (Kaggle license field). The paper says: "We publicly release FSboard … under a CC BY 4.0 license."
  - The CVPR 2025 supplement notes that CC does not supersede rights of publicity.
  - **Tier:** A. **commercial_ok:** yes. **video_redistrib_ok:** yes.
  - **Next action:** use it for fingerspelling handshape transitions, not lexical signs.
  - Sources: [Kaggle API record](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/googleai/fsboard); [arXiv 2407.15806](https://arxiv.org/html/2407.15806v1); [CVPR supplement](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content/CVPR2025/supplemental/Georg_FSboard_Over_3_CVPR_2025_supplemental.pdf).
- **Kaggle "Google – American Sign Language Fingerspelling Recognition" (`asl-fingerspelling`)**
  - Landmarks for the FSboard data. The Kaggle license object is `{'name': 'Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)'}`.
  - **Tier:** A.
  - Sources: [Kaggle competition](https://www.kaggle.com/competitions/asl-fingerspelling); FSboard card: "We previously hosted a Kaggle competition using MediaPipe Holistic landmarks for the FSboard data" ([Kaggle](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/googleai/fsboard)).
- **Wiktionary ASL lemmas**
  - **Type:** notation plus lexical entries.
  - **Size:** "Category:American Sign Language lemmas" has 271 pages (283 members including 12 part-of-speech subcategories).
  - **Format:** entry titles are notation strings. Examples:
    - `1@Chin-PalmBack X@NearChin-PalmBack` (RED)
    - `5@Chest-PalmBack FlatO@NearChest-PalmBack` (WHITE)
    - `1@InsideChesthigh-FingerAcross-1@InsideChesthigh-FingerAcross CirclesMidline-CirclesMidline`
  - The guideline: an entry name gives "a rough description of postures, holds, and moves of one or two hands". Each posture is `Handshape@Location-Facing`. Phoneme lists "may be extended without a vote".
  - **License:** the site footer reads "Definitions and other text are available under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike License". The site `rightsinfo` API returns "Creative Commons Attribution-Share Alike 4.0" (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/).
  - **SPDX:** CC-BY-SA-4.0 (or 3.0 for older revisions). **Tier:** A.
  - **Access:** MediaWiki API or the enwiktionary dump, not HTML scraping.
  - **commercial_ok:** yes, with ShareAlike.
  - **Next action:** high value. Parse the 271 titles straight into SignDesc (handshape, location, facing and movement tokens). This is the closest existing open notation to Marionet's IR.
  - Sources: [Wiktionary API category info](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Category:American_Sign_Language_lemmas); [Sign language entry guidelines](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/Wiktionary:Sign_language_entry_guidelines); [sample entry](https://en.wiktionary.org/wiki/1@Chin-PalmBack_X@NearChin-PalmBack); [siteinfo API](https://en.wiktionary.org/w/api.php?action=query&meta=siteinfo&siprop=rightsinfo).
- **Wikimedia Commons ASL posture photos** (User:Rodasmith, 2009)
  - Named in Wiktionary notation. Examples: "File:ASL 1@Chin-PalmBack.jpg", described as "The first posture of the ASL sign RED", and "File:ASL 5@Chest-PalmBack.jpg" (WHITE).
  - **License:** "CC BY-SA 3.0".
  - Category:American Sign Language has 280 files and 15 subcategories. "ASL handshapes (orange)" has 225 files, "Stokoe notation" 4 and "ASLwrite" 19.
  - **Tier:** A (images). **Next action:** use as handshape and posture reference images that go with the Wiktionary notation.
  - Source: [Commons API](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:American_Sign_Language).
- **Commons "Videos in American Sign Language"**
  - 161 files, mostly "Public domain" (White House and USDA interpreted speeches) and some "CC BY 3.0" (e.g., "Deaf culture intro.webm").
  - **Type:** continuous or interpreted video, not citation form.
  - **Tier:** A, but low lexical value. **Next action:** skip for SignDesc.
  - Source: [Commons category](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Category:Videos_in_American_Sign_Language).
- **J. Schuyler Long, *The Sign Language: A Manual of Signs*** (2nd ed. 1918)
  - **Wikisource:** a transcription with chapters ("The Sign Language/Chapter 2", "/Chapter 21", "/Introduction").
  - **Commons:** "Category:The Sign Language A Manual of Signs" has 47 files ("Plate I (The Sign Language 2nd ed. 1918).png" …), licensed "Public domain".
  - **Tier:** A (US public domain, published before 1929).
  - **Next action:** use the Wikisource text, which gives English descriptions of how each sign is made, as an LLM-to-SignDesc seed, with plates as images. See also Q3 for the historical-book records.
